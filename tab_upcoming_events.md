# Hackers Gambit 2026

## 🌐 International-Level Capture The Flag Competition

**Hackers Gambit 2026** is an international-level **Capture The Flag (CTF) cybersecurity competition** organized by **JCOE Cyber Sentinels**, the student-led cybersecurity club of **Jaihind College of Engineering, Kuran, India**.

The event is designed to bring together aspiring cybersecurity enthusiasts, ethical hackers, students, security researchers, and CTF players from across India and around the world. Participants will solve practical cybersecurity challenges across multiple domains, test their technical skills, and compete against other teams for the championship.

Building on the success of **Hackers Gambit 2025**, which attracted **1,785 participants**, the 2026 edition aims to reach **2,200+ participants** from **376+ colleges and institutions**.

# 🥇 Round 1 — 48-Hour Online CTF

### 🌍 Open for Participants Worldwide

**Date:** 2 October 2026 – 4 October 2026
**Duration:** 48 Hours
**Mode:** Online
**Eligibility:** Open to teams from around the world

The first round of Hackers Gambit 2026 is a **48-hour online CTF competition** designed to test participants across a wide range of cybersecurity domains.

Participants will compete by solving challenges, discovering vulnerabilities, analyzing evidence, breaking cryptographic systems, reversing binaries, and applying practical cybersecurity techniques.

The online qualifier will serve as the **selection round for the Grand Finale**.

### 🎯 Objective

The primary objective of Round 1 is to identify the strongest participating teams and shortlist the **Top 15 teams** for the offline Grand Finale.

### 🔐 Challenge Categories

Challenges may cover areas including:

* 🌐 Web Security
* 🔑 Cryptography
* 🔬 Reverse Engineering
* 🕵️ Digital Forensics
* 🔎 OSINT
* 🌐 Network Security
* 🐧 Linux & System Security
* 💻 Binary Exploitation
* 🧩 Miscellaneous
* 🔒 Other practical cybersecurity challenges

The exact challenge categories and difficulty levels may vary during the competition.

---

# 🏆 Round 2 — 24-Hour Offline Grand Finale

### 🔥 The Final Battle

**Date:** 30 October 2026 – 31 October 2026
**Duration:** 24 Hours
**Mode:** Offline
**Venue:** Jaihind College of Engineering, Kuran, Maharashtra, India
**Qualification:** Top 15 teams from Round 1

The **Grand Finale** is the final stage of Hackers Gambit 2026, where the best-performing teams from the online qualifier will compete in an intense **24-hour offline CTF**.

The finalists will compete head-to-head at **Jaihind College of Engineering, Kuran**, solving challenging cybersecurity problems and demonstrating their practical technical abilities.

The team achieving the highest score according to the official competition rules will be crowned the **Hackers Gambit 2026 Champion**.

---

# 🏅 Qualification

The competition follows a two-round structure:

**Round 1 → Online Qualifier → Top 15 Teams → Round 2 → Grand Finale**

The **Top 15 teams** from the online CTF will qualify for the offline Grand Finale.

Final rankings will be determined according to the official scoring and competition rules.
